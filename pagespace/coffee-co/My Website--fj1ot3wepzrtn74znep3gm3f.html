Ten minutes, watched the whole way

Every batch runs against a logged profile. If the curve drifts, the batch goes to staff coffee instead of your bag. Here's how a House Blend run reads.

  1. 0:00 Charge at 195°C Green coffee drops into a preheated drum and the temperature dives.
  2. 8:30 First crack Sugars caramelise and the beans audibly split. Airflow opens up.
  3. 10:20 Drop at 205°C Out to the cooling tray, stirred by hand, bagged once it's at room temperature.
