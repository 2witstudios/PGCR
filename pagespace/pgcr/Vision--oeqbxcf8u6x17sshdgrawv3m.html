Purpose

Build Pleasant Grove Coffee Roasting into a beloved neighborhood coffee company rooted in quality, hospitality, and local community.

Business Focus

  • Develop a reliable small-batch roasting operation with repeatable quality.
  • Launch a focused lineup of approachable coffees before expanding.
  • Sell through direct-to-consumer, local events, and carefully selected wholesale relationships.
  • Use disciplined costing, inventory control, and customer feedback to grow sustainably.

Operating Principles

  • Quality over unnecessary complexity.
  • Clear records and honest pricing.
  • Consistency customers can taste and trust.
  • Local relationships before aggressive scale.

Current Decisions to Make

  • Initial roast equipment, production capacity, and workspace.
  • Opening product lineup and bag sizes.
  • Pricing, packaging, sales channels, and launch date.
  • Food-safety, labeling, licensing, and fulfillment requirements.
