# Pleasant Grove Coffee Roasting — Business Plan

## Executive Summary

Pleasant Grove Coffee Roasting will be a local small-batch coffee roaster serving fresh, approachable coffee to households, offices, events, and selected wholesale partners. The first phase should prioritize product quality, repeatability, customer learning, and positive cash flow over rapid expansion.

## Customer Segments

- Local coffee drinkers who value fresh beans and neighborhood identity
- Gift buyers and event customers
- Small offices, churches, studios, and community organizations
- Cafes, restaurants, and specialty retailers that need a dependable local roaster

## Initial Offer

Start with a focused lineup: one approachable everyday coffee, one rotating single-origin or seasonal offering, and one decaf or darker-profile option if demand supports it. Offer whole bean first, with ground coffee as an operationally controlled add-on.

## Differentiation

- Fresh local roasting with transparent roast dates
- Friendly education without coffee snobbery
- Strong Pleasant Grove / Dallas community identity
- Direct relationships and responsive service
- Small-batch flexibility for seasonal and wholesale needs

## Revenue Channels

1. Direct online and local pickup orders
2. Pop-ups, markets, events, and community partnerships
3. Recurring subscriptions
4. Wholesale accounts
5. Gifts, bundles, and limited seasonal releases

## Key Metrics

- Gross margin by product and channel
- Repeat purchase rate
- Average order value
- Pounds roasted and sold per week
- Wholesale reorder rate
- Inventory days on hand
- Cash conversion and monthly contribution margin

## Open Questions

- What equipment and workspace are available?
- What local permits, labeling, and food-safety requirements apply?
- Which launch channels can be operated consistently?
- What minimum monthly sales volume supports the target income?

## 90-Day Priorities

- Validate the first three products with structured tastings.
- Build costing and pricing from green coffee through fulfillment.
- Document roast, packaging, order, and quality-control procedures.
- Secure initial customers and two to five pilot wholesale conversations.
- Run a small launch, collect feedback, and revise before scaling.
