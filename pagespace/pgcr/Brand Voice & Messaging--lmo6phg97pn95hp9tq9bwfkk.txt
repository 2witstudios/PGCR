# Brand Voice & Messaging

## Positioning

Pleasant Grove Coffee Roasting makes fresh, thoughtfully roasted coffee for people who want a great cup and a stronger connection to their neighborhood.

## Voice

- Warm, grounded, and welcoming
- Specific without being pretentious
- Confident but curious
- Community-minded and hospitable
- Honest about what we know and what we are still learning

## Avoid

- Coffee jargon without explanation
- Artificial luxury language
- Claims we cannot substantiate
- Generic “premium” language with no proof
- Talking down to newer coffee drinkers

## Messaging Pillars

1. **Freshness:** Roast dates, transparent process, and care from green bean to cup.
2. **Approachability:** Make specialty coffee easier to understand and enjoy.
3. **Place:** Pleasant Grove and Dallas roots, local relationships, and shared tables.
4. **Craft:** Small-batch attention, repeatable quality, and continual improvement.

## One-Sentence Description

Pleasant Grove Coffee Roasting is a neighborhood-minded Dallas roaster making fresh, approachable coffee in small batches.

## Proof to Build

- Customer testimonials and repeat-order data
- Roast and sourcing transparency
- Local partnerships and event participation
- Consistent product notes and quality records
