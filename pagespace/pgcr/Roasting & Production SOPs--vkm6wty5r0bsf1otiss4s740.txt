# Roasting & Production SOPs

## Purpose

Document repeatable procedures for green coffee intake, roasting, cooling, resting, packaging, quality control, and order fulfillment.

## Roast Record — Required Fields

- Date and operator
- Coffee / lot / supplier
- Batch size and target yield
- Charge temperature and environmental conditions
- Turning point, first crack, development time, drop temperature, and total roast time
- Roast outcome, defects, cupping notes, and next adjustment

## Production Flow

1. Confirm production plan, green inventory, packaging, and orders.
2. Inspect and weigh green coffee.
3. Roast to the approved profile and record actual data.
4. Cool fully and remove chaff according to equipment procedure.
5. Rest according to product standard before shipping when applicable.
6. Perform quality check and retain a sample.
7. Package with product name, roast date, lot information, net weight, and required labeling.
8. Update inventory and complete the batch record.

## Quality-Control Checks

- No visible packaging damage or seal failure
- Correct product, weight, label, and roast date
- Sample aroma and appearance within expected range
- Brew or cup sample recorded for each new profile and meaningful adjustment
- Complaints and corrective actions logged

## Safety and Compliance Notes

Add local requirements for permits, food handling, allergen statements, labeling, sanitation, fire safety, and traceability before commercial production. This page is an operating template, not legal advice.
