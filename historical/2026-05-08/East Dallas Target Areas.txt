# East Dallas Target Areas for AI Training

## Geographic Focus Areas

### Primary Target Cities:
- **Garland** - Large business community, growing tech presence
- **Mesquite** - Small business hub, manufacturing and logistics
- **Richardson** - Telecom corridor, but many non-tech roles
- **Plano (East)** - Corporate headquarters, marketing/sales teams
- **Rowlett** - Growing suburban business community
- **Sachse** - Small business owners, family operations
- **Wylie** - Expanding commercial areas
### Secondary Target Areas:
- **East Dallas Proper** - Local businesses, startups
- **Lakewood** - Professional services, consultants
- **Pleasant Grove** - Small business community
- **Balch Springs** - Industrial and service businesses

### Further Suburban Expansion Areas:
- **Terrell** - Manufacturing hub, growing retail sector, healthcare facilities
- **Greenville** - Logistics center, small business community, educational institutions
- **Quinlan** - Rural business community, agricultural services, local entrepreneurs
- **Commerce** - University town (Texas A&M Commerce-Commerce), student market, local businesses
- **Royse City** - Rapid growth area, new developments, expanding business community
- **Fate** - Fast-growing residential area with new commercial development
- **Rockwall** - Affluent suburban area, corporate offices, healthcare hub
- **Forney** - Family-oriented business community, retail growth, service businesses
- **Canton** - Tourism-based economy, First Monday Trade Days, local retail services
